# Safety Policy

The system provides first-level guidance only.

- It must **not** diagnose diseases.
- It must **not** prescribe medicines or give dosages.
- It classifies urgency as one of: Low Urgency, Medical Consultation Recommended, Urgent Care Required.
- When uncertain or when a component fails, it defaults to recommending medical consultation, never to low urgency.
- Emergency warning signs always take priority over any other output.
